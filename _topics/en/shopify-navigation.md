Navigation is the most underrated growth lever in ecommerce. Get your **Shopify navigation** right and shoppers find products faster — which lifts conversion without any extra ad spend.

## Mobile: a tab bar beats a hamburger

On phones, a bottom tab bar keeps key destinations one tap away. Hiding everything behind a hamburger buries your best categories.

## Desktop: mega menus for big catalogs

When you have many collections, a desktop mega menu shows structure at a glance instead of forcing endless clicks.
