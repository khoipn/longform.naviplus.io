Your theme sets the ceiling for speed, trust, and conversion. Here's how to choose a **Shopify theme** without overthinking it.

## Prioritize speed over flash

A fast theme beats a flashy one every time. Heavy sliders and autoplay video look nice in demos but cost you sales on mobile.

## Make sure navigation is flexible

Pick a theme that doesn't fight you on menus. You'll want room for a clear mobile tab bar and a desktop mega menu — the backbone of a store that guides shoppers to checkout.

## Don't pay for features you won't use

Most premium theme features go untouched. Start lean; add only what moves revenue.
